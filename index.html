                                  About Vulforce

                                  Vulforce is a free, automated digest of the vulnerabilities attackers are exploiting right now and the security news around them. It sorts everything into red, orange and green, so an analyst can see in a minute what needs attention first.

                                  Who is behind it

                                  Hi, I'm Markus. I study software engineering and work in security. I built Vulforce because I wanted one page that tells me each morning what attackers are using and what to look at first.

                                  It's a personal project and I keep improving it. If you spot a mistake or have an idea, write to me by email or find me on GitHub.

                                  Where the data comes from

                                  CISA KEV catalogueWhich vulnerabilities are confirmed exploited, the required action, the due date, ransomware use and the weakness type.
                                  FIRST EPSSThe predicted chance that a vulnerability is exploited in the next 30 days.
                                  CVE Program recordsCVSS severity, taken from the vendor's score or, if there is none, from CISA's.
                                  Ransomware.liveVictims claimed by ransomware groups on their leak sites. Vulforce keeps only counts per country, group and sector, and shows no victim names.
                                  XRecent posts from 20 security accounts, shown on the Feed tab unchanged and linked to the original post.
                                  News feedsBleepingComputer, The Record, The Hacker News and CISA advisories. Only the headline, date and link are kept.

                                  Vulforce is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of these organisations. Headlines belong to their publishers.

                                  How the colours are decided

                                  Every item gets a score from 0 to 100 from fixed rules. No AI ranks anything, and each item shows the rules that produced its score.

                                  How AI is used

                                  Text marked AI is written by Claude, an AI model from Anthropic: the short story summaries, the Feed tab's summary of X posts, the sector, country and ATT&CK tags, some actor and malware names, and the note on what matters today. It is based only on the headline and the short description each feed provides, so it can be incomplete or wrong. Always open the linked source. Scores and colours never come from AI.

                                  How often it updates

                                  The sources are checked every 2 hours. An open page re-reads the data every 10 minutes, and the time of the data is shown at the top right.

                                  Limits you should know

                                  • It ranks general risk. It knows nothing about your systems, so a red item may not affect you and a green one might.
                                  • News is scored by keywords in the headline, which will sometimes misjudge a story.
                                  • Very new vulnerabilities often have no CVSS or EPSS score yet. They are scored without it.
                                  • The map shows where victims are reported to be, not where attackers are. Ransomware figures are the groups' own claims and are not verified.
                                  • Threat actor names come from a fixed watchlist, so an actor that is not on it will not appear.
                                  • The feed summary is written by AI from X posts. It can merge or misread stories, so open the source posts before relying on it.
                                  • The site is provided as is. Confirm against the original source before you act on anything here.
